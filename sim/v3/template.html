What am I looking at?

This is a model story, not observed taxi behaviour. Teal dots represent vacant taxis, amber dots hired trips, and violet dots repositioning. Dark dots approach a pickup first. Dots follow real OSM roads, but their speed is chosen to preserve the model's arrival times. Taxi identities and individual paths are illustrative.

Rain comes from your settings. “More bookings”, “Drivers stop” and “Both” are alternative assumptions. Hired trips and abandoned requests are simulated, not measured demand or lost passengers. The engine uses 30-minute rain slots; the radar layer interpolates the synthetic storm for display. Starting supply and the normal line use the frozen typical weekday/weekend half-hour pattern, averaged across months at normal feed coverage. The public feed covers the whole market.

The model failed its fixed realism check: its best setting passed 3 of 10 real storms. Use this page to explore assumptions, not to make operating decisions. Major roads only; cell centres snap to this connected road network. 1× playback means one simulated minute per second. Grey fading dots leave service; returning dots fade in.
