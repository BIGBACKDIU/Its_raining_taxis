# 2019 exogenous calendar inputs

Public holidays: Ministry of Manpower, 4 April 2018, archived by the National Archives of Singapore:
https://www.nas.gov.sg/archivesonline/data/pdfdoc/20180404008/Press%20Release%20-%20Public%20Holidays%20for%202019.pdf
The following-Monday rule supplies the three observed holidays, as in the existing calendar encoding.

School vacations: Ministry of Education press release, 15 August 2018, reproduced verbatim under its
Ministry of Education Press Releases byline:
https://wp.kiasuparents.com/kiasu/news/school-terms-and-holidays-for-2019/
Original MOE URL https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/press-releases/20180815-school-terms-and-holidays-for-2019
currently presents a bot check. The web index exposes the authored release; direct HTTP retrieval of the mirror returns a missing-page response, so no such response is saved as source data. The year-end dates
are also in the government school's September 2019 circular:
https://www.jieminpri.moe.edu.sg/files/Jieminite%20Link/Jlink_T4_2019.pdf

Only the four primary/secondary vacation ranges are encoded, matching the frozen step-22 feature's
convention; single school-only holidays are not added. The frozen 2021-2026 calendar stays untouched.
These are scheduled calendar inputs, not fitted statistics or taxi/weather outcomes.
